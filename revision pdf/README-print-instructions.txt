======================================================================
IMPORTANT
======================================================================

* PRINT IN COLOUR. The colour coding is not decoration — it is how you
  find things on the page. Red boxes are the exam traps, purple are the
  memory hooks, green are the confirmed facts. In black and white they
  all look the same and the sheets become much harder to use.

* DO NOT let anyone "fit to page" or "shrink to printable area". The
  pages are built to exact A4. Scaling them down makes the text smaller
  than it needs to be, which is the whole problem these sheets were
  rebuilt to solve.

* PRINT ONCE. Write your own notes in the margins all year — whatever
  your professor stresses, whatever comes in a PYQ. By March those
  handwritten margins will be worth more than the printed text, and you
  cannot get them back if you reprint.


======================================================================
HOW TO USE THEM
======================================================================

  Weekdays   Learn from the full notes on the website. Write in your
             notebook as usual.

  Saturday   This week's topics. Test yourself from the sheet FIRST,
             then go back to the website only for what you got wrong.

  Sunday     Older units. The sheet is the main tool here — you are
             refreshing, not learning. Do the blank-fill drill at the
             end of the unit with the answers covered.

  Spacing    Revise each unit again at about +1 week, +3 weeks,
             +6 weeks, then monthly.

  Every card is numbered, and each page header tells you which cards it
  holds (e.g. "PAGE 3 OF 8 · CARDS 15-21"), so the app and the paper
  always refer to the same thing.

  The same sheets are inside the app under "Rapid Revision", where the
  answers can be blurred for self-testing and your score is tracked.
