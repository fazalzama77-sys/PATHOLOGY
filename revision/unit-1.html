Named men — always asked
Rudolf Virchow — Father of modern/cellular pathology ("omnis cellula e cellula"); added functio laesa. Morgagni — organ pathology (De Sedibus). Hippocrates — humoral theory. Celsus — first four cardinal signs. Bichat — tissue pathology.

2 · Causes of disease

INTRINSIC (from within)
  • Genetic — lethal, sub-lethal genes; chromosomal (freemartin 60,XX/XY chimera)
  • Immunological — hypersensitivity, autoimmunity, immunodeficiency
  • Age, sex, species, breed predisposition
EXTRINSIC (from outside)
  • Physical — trauma, heat, cold, radiation, electricity, pressure
  • Chemical — poisons, toxins, drugs, heavy metals
  • Nutritional — deficiency, excess, imbalance
  • Biological — virus, bacteria, fungi, protozoa, helminths, arthropods, prions
One-liner A predisposing factor makes disease likely; a determining/specific cause produces it.

9 · Embolism

  • Thromboembolism — commonest (~99%)
  • Fat — long-bone fracture, fatty liver rupture
  • Air / gas — IV injection, decompression ("bends")
  • Bacterial / septic — valvular endocarditis → embolic nephritis
  • Parasitic — Strongylus vulgaris (horse, cranial mesenteric a.), Dirofilaria
  • Neoplastic, amniotic fluid, foreign body, hair, tissue
Direction rule Venous emboli lodge in the LUNG; arterial (left heart) emboli lodge in brain, kidney, spleen, limbs. A paradoxical embolus crosses a septal defect. Saddle thrombus at aortic trifurcation = classic feline (HCM).

11 · Reversible cell injury (degenerations)

Hypoxia→↓ ATP→ Na+/K+ ATPase fails→ Na+ & H2O in→Cellular swelling
  • Cloudy swelling = the earliest & commonest reversible change; organ pale, swollen, heavy, dull ("parboiled"), cut surface bulges.
  • Hydropic / vacuolar degeneration — larger clear vacuoles
  • Hyaline degeneration — glassy, pink, homogeneous (Russell bodies, hyaline casts, Zenker's)
  • Mucoid / mucinous degeneration — goblet cell excess
  • Zenker's (waxy) degeneration — hyaline change of skeletal muscle: white muscle disease, blackleg, capture myopathy
Point of no return Irreversibility = membrane damage + mitochondrial permeability transition + massive Ca2+ influx. Leaked enzymes (ALT, AST, CK, LDH) are the clinical marker.

  • Granulation tissue = new capillaries + fibroblasts + loose ECM + inflammatory cells. Red, granular, bleeds easily. It is NOT granuloma.
  • Tensile strength: ~10% at 1 week → maximum ~70–80% by 3 months. Never 100%.
  • Vitamin C — hydroxylation of proline & lysine (collagen cross-linking). Zinc — epithelialisation. Copper — lysyl oxidase.
Growth factors VEGF = most potent angiogenic. TGF-β = most potent fibrogenic (collagen ↑, collagenase ↓) and anti-inflammatory. PDGF = fibroblast & smooth-muscle migration. EGF/TGF-α = epithelialisation. FGF-2 = angiogenesis + granulation.
Complications & cell types Dehiscence · keloid · exuberant granulation tissue = "PROUD FLESH", classic on the distal limb of the HORSE · contracture · adhesions. Delayed by infection (most important), foreign body, movement, poor blood supply, corticosteroids, protein/vit-C/zinc deficiency.
Labile (skin, gut, marrow) — always dividing · Stable (liver, kidney, pancreas) — divide on demand · Permanent (neuron, cardiac & skeletal muscle) — heal by SCAR only.

25 · AMYLOIDOSIS (fits here — always asked with stains)

  • Structure = β-pleated sheet fibrils, non-branching, 7.5–10 nm; plus P component (serum amyloid P).
  • AA (secondary/reactive) — from SAA; commonest in animals; chronic suppuration, TB, hyperimmunised horses.
  • AL (primary) — immunoglobulin light chain; plasma-cell tumours.
  • Organ order: KIDNEY (glomerulus) first → liver → spleen → adrenal → intestine. Renal amyloid → protein-losing nephropathy.
  • Sago spleen = nodular (follicular) · Lardaceous/bacon spleen = diffuse red pulp.
Stains ★ Congo red → salmon/orange-red in ordinary light and APPLE-GREEN BIREFRINGENCE under polarised light (the single most-asked fact). Methyl/crystal violet → metachromatic pink-red. Thioflavin T → fluorescence. Gross Lugol's iodine + dilute H2SO4 → mahogany brown → blue-violet.
